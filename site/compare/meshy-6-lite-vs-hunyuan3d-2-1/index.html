Meshy 6 Lite vs Hunyuan3D-2.1 for 3D printing (same 6 prompts)

  • Answer: on our v0.2 rubric with locked prompts T01–T06, open-weights Hunyuan3D-2.1 mean ~83.2 (6/6 watertight, 0 hard fails) beats free-plan Meshy 6 Lite mean ~77.9 (6/6 watertight; T05 slice hard-fail).
  • Meshy here is Meshy 6 Lite on the free plan (untextured image-to-3D, 60 free credits, $0) — not flagship Meshy 7.1. Meshy T01 scored 99.5 and nearly ties Hunyuan3D-2 (98.2).
  • HY2.1 main-table scores for T01/T04/T05/T06 include Space rembg on our off-white refs (thin ground plate). Matted RGBA re-test removed the plate on 4/4: T01 86.6, T04 98.2, T05 72.2, T06 95.6. Main scorecard table unchanged (same protocol); retests disclosed separately.
  • Printable ≠ matches prompt. Hunyuan3D-2.1 T03 scored 96.7 but looked like a hex prism / wrong shape — the rubric has no matches-prompt column yet (planned).
  • Free downloads: Meshy 10 untextured Lite/mo; HY2.1 Space yes (rate-limited). See /costs Free downloads?. Full tables: scorecard · HY2 vs 2.1 · open data · methodology.
