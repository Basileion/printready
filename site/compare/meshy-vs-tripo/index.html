Meshy vs Tripo for 3D printing (printability head-to-head)

  • Correction 2026-11-07: this page first said Tripo H2.5 had no watertight meshes, a mean of ~26.2 and that Meshy won by a wide margin. That came from a bug in our scorer: its cleanup step deleted zero-area sliver triangles from closed Tripo meshes, which opened holes. Fixed and re-scored; see the methodology change log.
  • Printability at $0: a near tie. Tripo H2.5 free export mean ~78.3 (5/6 watertight; 1 hard-fail, T04 not watertight) vs free-plan Meshy 6 Lite mean ~77.9 (6/6 watertight; 1 hard-fail, T05 slice). A 0.4-point gap on 6 prompts is noise, not a winner. Per prompt Tripo leads 4 (T02, T03, T05, T06) and Meshy leads 2 (T01, T04). Same locked prompts T01–T06, rubric v0.2. This is not Meshy 7.1 and not newer paid Tripo models.
  • Free downloads: Meshy 10 untextured Lite/mo (CC BY 4.0 — sell with credit). Tripo 15 exports, H2.5 only (non-commercial). Checked Meshy 2026-10-31 · Tripo 2026-11-06 on /costs/.
  • Who to try first at $0: selling prints? Meshy 6 Lite: free models are CC BY 4.0 (sell with credit), and it went 6/6 watertight. Personal prints? Either works; Tripo gives more free exports (15 vs 10) and scored about the same, but free Tripo is non-commercial. Check thin walls on Tripo T05-type parts (wall p5 0.14 mm).
  • Printable ≠ matches prompt. Matches prompt? reviewed for both: Meshy T03 = no (tall hex coupler); Tripo 3 yes / 3 partial (T03 nut ~2x M8 thickness; T05 pockets, not 3 vertical slots; T06 missing center drainage hole). No Tripo row is a wrong object. Every scored T06 planter (any generator) is partial for the missing drainage hole (RT-20261107-70).
  • Also see: Meshy 6 Lite vs Hunyuan3D-2.1 · Tripo H2.5 scorecard · scores.csv.
