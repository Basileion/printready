Hunyuan3D-2 vs Hunyuan3D-2.1 for 3D printing (same 6 prompts)

  • Answer: both are open-weights Tencent models. On our v0.2 rubric with locked prompts T01–T06, main-table means are ~82.2 (HY2, 5/6 watertight) vs ~83.2 (HY2.1, 6/6 watertight).
  • HY2.1 main-table scores for T01/T04/T05/T06 include Space rembg on our off-white refs, which produced a thin ground plate (wall ~0.29 mm, hull fill ~7%). Correction re-test with local matted RGBA removed the plate on 4/4 and changed scores: T01 86.6, T04 98.2, T05 72.2, T06 95.6. Main scorecard table unchanged (same protocol for every generator); retests are disclosed separately.
  • Printable ≠ matches prompt. Hunyuan3D-2.1 prompt T03 scored 96.7 (highest AI score here) but looked like a hex prism / wrong shape — the rubric does not yet have a matches-prompt column (planned).
  • Free downloads: yes on both public Spaces (rate-limited). See /costs Free downloads?. Full tables: scorecard · open data · methodology.
