Fair questions makers ask

Straight answers, including where we’re still thin.

“Meshy already has a printability check.”

It does, and it’s useful. It only checks Meshy’s own output. We run the same locked prompts through several generators and score them with one neutral rubric, so you can compare before you pay for credits.

“I just budget 20 minutes in Blender per model.”

Fair. The scorecard shows which generators need that cleanup and which don’t: non-manifold edges, holes, and walls under 0.4 mm show up per prompt, per tool.

“Can I check my own file?”

Not yet. Today you can download our scorer inputs and results from open data. For functional parts with tolerances, CAD (OpenSCAD, Fusion) still beats any AI generator.

Honest limits: one generator scored so far (Hunyuan3D-2, 6 prompts). Hollow-skin and orientation checks are on the rubric roadmap.
