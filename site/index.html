Fair questions makers ask

Straight answers, including where we’re still thin.

“Meshy already has a printability check.”

It does, and it’s useful. It only checks Meshy’s own output. We score every generator with one neutral rubric on the same locked prompts. Today that’s Hunyuan3D-2 only; Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, TRELLIS and Hitem3D are queued, so side-by-side comparisons are coming, not live yet.

“I just budget 20 minutes in Blender per model.”

Fair. The scorecard shows where that cleanup is needed: non-manifold edges, holes, and walls under 0.4 mm show up per prompt (Hunyuan3D-2 today, more generators as they’re added).

“Can I check my own file?”

Not yet. Today you can download our scorer inputs and results from open data. For functional parts with tolerances, CAD (OpenSCAD, Fusion) still beats any AI generator.

“Just got an A1. Is Meshy (not yet scored) worth paying for?”

We can’t say yet: Meshy is queued, not scored. What we can show while Meshy is queued: Pro is $20/mo (about $0.20 per asset, not per printable model) and the free tier is 100 credits/month. Compare plans, and get the Friday email when Meshy’s scores land.

“Can I sell prints of AI models on Etsy or MakerWorld?”

Depends on the plan. Free tiers of Tripo and PrintPal (both queued, not scored yet) are non-commercial; Meshy’s free tier, also not yet scored, is CC BY 4.0 (credit Meshy); Hunyuan3D-2’s license excludes the EU, UK and South Korea. License terms per tool, with vendor links. Read them before you sell.

“Why STL? The generators give me GLB.”

Most generators export GLB; your slicer wants STL or 3MF. We score the raw mesh the generator hands you, so holes and thin walls show up before you convert and slice it.
