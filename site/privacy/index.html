Privacy · updated 2026-10-31

Privacy, in plain words.

PrintReady AI is a free printability benchmark for AI 3D generators. We collect as little as we can: your email only if you give it to us, and anonymous page events with no cookies.

What we collect and why

Newsletter email. If you sign up, your email address goes to Kit (ConvertKit), our email provider, with double opt-in: nothing is sent until you confirm. Kit stores your address and standard email data (opens, clicks) so we can send PrintReady Weekly. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Legal basis: your consent.

Anonymous usage events, no cookies. A small first-party script (grow.js) sends page views and clicks on key links and the signup button to our own Cloudflare Worker, which stores them in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. Each event holds the site, page path, link label, UTM campaign tags and the referring page. It does not include your IP address, the email you type, or any cookie or user ID. Legal basis: legitimate interest in knowing which pages help people.

On your device. If you arrive from a link with UTM tags, those tags are kept in your browser’s local storage (grow:utm, not a cookie) for up to 7 days so a later click can be attributed to that campaign. Clearing site data removes it.

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

What we don’t do

No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics cookies, no selling or renting your data. Sponsors, if we ever have any, never receive subscriber emails.

How long we keep it

Email: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Anonymous events: as long as Cloudflare Analytics Engine retains them (about three months).

Your rights and deleting your data

You can unsubscribe from any email in one click. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email basileion.ops@gmail.com from the address concerned with the subject “Delete my data” (or “Access my data”). We remove you from Kit and confirm by email. If you are in the EU/UK you also have the right to object, restrict processing, data portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes. Questions: basileion.ops@gmail.com. See also our terms.
