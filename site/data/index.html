Reproduce a score

  1. Get the scorer: pipeline/score_v02.py (rubric v0.2; open source; uses trimesh + OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 and the tree-support profile in pipeline/profiles).
  2. Download a mesh, gunzip T01.glb.gz, and check it against SHA256SUMS.
  3. Run the scorer and compare with that prompt's JSON.

To regenerate a mesh: the locked prompt plus ", single object centered on plain white background, product photo, soft diffuse light" goes to FLUX.1-schnell (seed 1234), and the image goes to the public Hunyuan3D-2 Space (shape only, seed 1234, 30 steps, guidance 5.0, octree 256). See gen_free.py and PROMPTS.md.
