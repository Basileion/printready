Reproducible scoring

Methodology

Rubric v0.2 (since 2026-10-31). Every mesh is normalized so its longest bounding-box edge is 50 mm, turned to its declared up-axis (glTF/GLB files are Y-up; STL is Z-up), then scored 0–100. Hard fail: if the mesh is not watertight or the slicer fails, the score is capped at 39.

Weight table (v0.2)

CheckWeightPass bar
Watertight20trimesh is_watertight after dropping index-degenerate export-padding faces
Manifold15Every edge shared by exactly 2 faces
Non-manifold edge count100 ideal; half points if ≤5
Self-intersections100 overlapping bodies / faces
Wall thickness (ray p5)105th-percentile inward ray thickness, 400 seeded samples: ≥0.8 mm full, ≥0.4 mm 40%, else 0 (0 if not a closed volume)
Hull fill (hollow skin)10Volume ÷ convex-hull volume: ≥20% full, ≤5% zero, linear between
Bed contact (flat base)10Downward faces within 0.2 mm of the bed in the declared pose: ≥100 mm² full, ≤10 mm² zero, log-linear between
Tree-support volume10OrcaSlicer tree(auto) supports at 45°; support extrusion ÷ all extrusion: ≤5% full, ≥35% zero, linear between
Slice success5OrcaSlicer emits G-code (exit 0)
Print time0 (n/a)Reported, not scored until a weekly median exists
Total100Hard fail still caps at 39

Geometric overhang % (faces more than 45° past vertical, off the bed) is published in each JSON as a cross-check, not scored, because the slicer's real support volume measures the same thing more directly. Code: pipeline/score_v02.py.

v0.1 weights (history, 2026-10-30)

Watertight 20 · Manifold 15 · Non-manifold edges 10 · Self-intersections 10 · Thin walls 10 · Slice success 20 · Print time 5 · Support volume 10. v0.1 sliced with supports off and auto-orient on, so support (10) and print time (5) were full points for every mesh that sliced. v0.1 scores stay downloadable: scores_v0.1.csv.

Slicer profile (v0.2)

  • OrcaSlicer 2.4.2
  • Machine: Bambu Lab A1 · 0.4 mm nozzle
  • Process: 0.20 mm Standard @BBL A1 + tree(auto) supports, 45° threshold (profile JSON)
  • Pose: declared up-axis, auto-orient off
  • Filament: Generic PLA @BBL A1

Profile names are recorded in each score JSON. Prompt pack and slicer versions are frozen per weekly archive.

Export padding (not holes)

Some generator Space GLB exports include large numbers of index-degenerate, zero-area filler triangles. Those are reported as export padding and removed before watertight and manifold checks. They are not treated as holes.

Matches prompt? (disclosed human call, not in the 100-pt rubric)

Since 2026-11-07 the scorecard and open data include a Matches prompt? column: yes / partial / no. It is a disclosed human review of the published thumb against the locked FLUX.1-schnell reference for that prompt (seed 1234). It is not part of the 100-pt printability rubric and does not change any printability score.

  • yes — clearly the requested object
  • partial — recognizable but major defects (missing handle, ground plate, sideways, wrong proportions, wrong slot count, multi-body debris)
  • no — clearly the wrong object (e.g. long hex prism instead of a flat M8 nut)

Printability means on compare pages and the scorecard stay geometric/slicer-only. Rows with matches_prompt=no are excluded from any “best / highest” claim copy. Flagship honesty case: Hunyuan3D-2.1 T03 scored 96.7 but is no. Data: matches_prompt.csv and columns on scores.csv.

What we do not score (yet)

Nothing has been physically printed yet. Every number comes from OrcaSlicer 2.4.2 and trimesh geometry checks on the Bambu A1 / PLA profile, not from real prints. A pass means the slicer and geometry checks pass, not that a print succeeded.

Texture beauty, game-engine retopo, multi-color painting, or “looks cool on Sketchfab.” Those are someone else’s leaderboard.
