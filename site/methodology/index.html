Methodology

Every mesh is normalized so its longest bounding-box edge is 50 mm, then scored 0–100. Hard fail: if the mesh is not watertight or the slicer fails, the score is capped at 39.

Rubric v2 diagnostics (preview, 2026-10-31)

Public benchmarks found that almost every AI mesh slices, so slice success says little. Rubric v2 measures the defects makers actually hit. These numbers are measured, not yet scored: v0.1 scores above are unchanged.

  • Hull fill %: mesh volume ÷ convex-hull volume. Very low values flag hollow skins.
  • Ray wall p5: 5th-percentile inward ray thickness from 400 surface samples (mm, 50 mm scale).
  • First-layer area: downward faces within 0.2 mm of the bed, in mm². Shown for the Z-up pose v0.1 sliced and the glTF Y-up pose.

Profile names are recorded in each score JSON. Prompt pack and slicer versions are frozen per weekly archive.

Export padding (not holes)

Some generator Space GLB exports include large numbers of index-degenerate, zero-area filler triangles. Those are reported as export padding and removed before watertight and manifold checks. They are not treated as holes.

What we do not score (yet)

Texture beauty, game-engine retopo, multi-color painting, or “looks cool on Sketchfab.” Those are someone else’s leaderboard.
