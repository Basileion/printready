Terms, in plain words.

PrintReady AI publishes printability scores for meshes from AI 3D generators. It is information, not advice or a guarantee that any model will print.

What the scores are

Scores come from the published rubric: mesh checks plus a slicer run on a fixed printer profile. They are slicer-measured, not physically printed (yet). Results depend on the prompt, the generator version on the day we ran it, and the export settings listed with each run. Your results can differ.

Prices and plans

The costs page copies each vendor’s own pricing page on the date shown. Vendors change prices and terms without notice, so check the vendor’s page before you pay for credits. We are not affiliated with any generator we score.

Using our data

The score and cost datasets on the data page are licensed CC BY 4.0: reuse them, including commercially, with a link back to PrintReady AI. Meshes produced by a generator stay under that generator’s own license terms.

No warranty

The site and data are provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for losses from using them, including failed prints, wasted filament or credits bought on the strength of a score.

Corrections and contact
