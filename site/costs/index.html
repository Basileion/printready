What it costs to get a printable AI 3D model

  • Is it worth paying for credits? Here is what each plan costs, what the free tier gives you, and whether you can sell prints.
  • Cost per model below is the vendor's allowance divided by the price. It is not cost per printable model. We only have printability scores for the open-source Hunyuan3D-2 and Hunyuan3D-2.1 so far (5 of 6 and 6 of 6 watertight); the paid tools are queued for the same locked prompts.
  • Selling on Etsy or MakerWorld? Free tiers of Tripo and PrintPal are non-commercial; Meshy's free tier is CC BY 4.0 (credit Meshy). Read the license link before you sell.
  • Own a Bambu printer? MakerWorld's MakerLab Image to 3D runs Tripo, Hitem3D, Hunyuan 3D, Meshy and Rodin with 2 free generations per account, then roughly $0.21 to $0.39 per generation on the $3 credit pack, with no subscription.
