What it costs to get a printable AI 3D model

  • Is it worth paying for credits? Here is what each plan costs, what the free tier gives you, whether you can download on free, and whether you can sell prints.
  • Cost per model below is the vendor's allowance divided by the price. It is not cost per printable model. Printability scores so far: Hunyuan3D-2, Hunyuan3D-2.1 and Meshy 6 Lite (free plan, not flagship 7.1; 6 of 6 watertight, T05 slice hard-fail). Head-to-head: Hunyuan3D-2 vs 2.1 · Meshy vs Tripo (docs + Meshy scores; Tripo queued). Tripo, Rodin, Hitem3D, MakeIt3D and Meshy 7.1 are queued for the same locked prompts.
  • Free downloads (short version): Hunyuan Space yes; Meshy 10 Lite/mo; Tripo 15 exports, H2.5 only; Hitem3D ~7 trial; MakerLab 2 free; PrintPal 10 Draft STL; MakeIt3D free/packs yes (CC BY-NC gallery).
  • Selling on Etsy or MakerWorld? Free tiers of Tripo and PrintPal are non-commercial; Meshy's free tier is CC BY 4.0 (credit Meshy); MakeIt3D free/packs are CC BY-NC. Read the license link before you sell.
  • Own a Bambu printer? MakerWorld's MakerLab Image to 3D runs Tripo, Hitem3D, Hunyuan 3D, Meshy and Rodin with 2 free generations per account, then roughly $0.21 to $0.39 per generation on the $3 credit pack, with no subscription.
